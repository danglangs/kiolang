## Now

Compiled-only kio covers the whole language and every capability. What real apps needed is built, and so are the review and eval layers of direction A (P1 authority diff, S1 host-injected headers, E1 and E2 models-writing-kio evals: 45 tasks): the HTTP client (H16, H16b), SQLite (H17), `send` answering at once (H18) and `Hmac` for signed webhooks (H22), from the concurrency proposal (decided). H16-H19 and H17b are built and reviewed (fixes merged); speed work is under way with C8c: the plan is written (docs/proposals/allocation-speed.md: attribution, unit costs, nine ranked tasks C8c1-C8c9 pinned in compiler-todo.md; per-loop fuel waits on the maintainer) and C8c1, C8c2 and C8c8 are in (unboxed `Option`/`Result`/one-slot enums and structs of at most 4 fields, borrowed loop variables and match bindings: `Map` word count 28 to 24 ms, `HashMap` 23 to 19, json.kio 63 to 58, tree 90 to 68; C8c3-C8c6 next). Compiled code is 1.1-2.3x Rust on compute and 2.1-7.3x where it allocates (roadmap.md "Speed today"); after C8c1-C8c8 the proposal predicts tree 1.6x, fib 1.9-2.3x, word counts ~3x (parity with Rust's own `BTreeMap`), json.kio ~5x, with the reasons where 2x is out of reach. The differential harness runs 509 programs both ways (506 agree, 3 excluded by limits, 0 leaked) and the generator 250 more per CI run.
